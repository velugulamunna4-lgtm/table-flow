# Table Flow · 3-Minute Restaurant Owner Pitch & Demo Walkthrough

> **Goal:** Show a restaurant owner or recruiter within 180 seconds that Table Flow turns tables 14 minutes faster, cuts order mistakes to zero, and lifts dessert/beverage sales without hiring more staff.

---

## ⏱️ Preparation (30 seconds before presentation)
Open two side-by-side browser windows (or phone + laptop):
- **Window A (Left - Customer Mobile View):** `http://localhost:3000/t/7` (Set DevTools to iPhone 14 / 390px or open on phone)
- **Window B (Right - Kitchen Display System):** `http://localhost:3000/kitchen` (Full desktop view in dark mode)

---

## 🎙️ The 3-Minute Live Script

### Minute 0:00 – 0:45 | The Customer Friction & First Impression
**Speaker says:**
> *"Namaste / Hello! Every Friday evening, your guests wait 8 minutes just to get a physical menu, another 10 minutes to wave down a captain, and orders often get misheard when the restaurant is buzzing.*
>
> *With Table Flow, the guest sits at Table 7, points their phone camera at the acrylic tent card, and this happens instantly—no app download required."*

**Action on Window A (Customer Phone):**
1. Show the warm, appetizing header: **"Welcome! Pick what you like."** and Table 7 badge.
2. Tap the **Language Switcher** in top right: switch from English to **Telugu (తెలుగు)** or **Hindi (हिंदी)**. Point out how dish names and descriptions instantly translate.
3. Tap **"Pure Veg"** filter pill — show how non-veg items filter out smoothly. Reset to **"All Items"**.
4. Tap **"+ Add"** on *Hyderabadi Chicken Dum Biryani* — highlight how it morphs into a tactile `- 1 +` stepper with spring feedback. Add *Mango Lassi*.
5. Show the bottom sticky glass bar: **"2 items · ₹540 · View order"**.

---

### Minute 0:45 – 1:30 | The Real-Time Synchronization Magic
**Speaker says:**
> *"Notice the chef instructions feature. The guest adds 'Extra onions, make it spicy'. Now watch Window B on the right—this is your head chef's kitchen display."*

**Action:**
1. On Window A, tap **"View order"** to slide up the smooth bottom sheet.
2. Tap **"Place Order Now"**.
3. **Point to Window B (Kitchen):** Instantly, a new ticket arrives under **"1. Incoming New"** with Table 7, the biryani, and the note highlighted in bold terracotta!
4. On Window B (Kitchen), tap **"Accept & Cook"**.
5. **Look back at Window A (Customer):** The customer's screen immediately transitions from *Sent* to a pulsing orange flame: **"Cooking in Wok · Ready in ~15 mins"**.

---

### Minute 1:30 – 2:15 | The Waiting Experience & Kitchen Control
**Speaker says:**
> *"Normally, while waiting 15 minutes for dum biryani, guests get impatient or ask waiters 'Where is my food?'. Instead, Table Flow keeps them entertained and driving more revenue."*

**Action:**
1. On Window A, tap **"While you wait, play a fun game!"**.
2. Show the 3 mini-games: tap **"Food Memory Match"**, flip 2 cards, and show **"Spice Points"** increasing to unlock **"10% off dessert"**.
3. Switch back to Window B (Kitchen): tap **"86 / Sold Out"**. Toggle *Nawabi Murgh Malai Tikka* off.
4. On Window A (Customer Menu), show that the dish immediately turns grey with **"Not available right now"** overlay so customers can't order what you don't have.
5. On Kitchen, tap **"Mark Ready for Table"** — customer's tracker turns green **"Plated & Ready to Serve!"**.

---

### Minute 2:15 – 3:00 | Effortless Bill Splitting & Counter Checkout
**Speaker says:**
> *"At the end of a group meal, splitting the bill is usually a nightmare for the waiter. Here, friends don't need calculators."*

**Action:**
1. On Window A, tap **"View Bill & Pay"**.
2. Show the itemized bill with 5% GST and Service Charge cleanly itemized.
3. Tap **"Split Equally"** — tap the `+` stepper to 3 people: show individual share calculated to ₹615/person.
4. Tap **"Split by Items"** — tap the biryani to claim it.
5. Tap **"UPI / QR"** — show the live dynamic UPI QR code ready for Google Pay or PhonePe.
6. Tap **"Pay Now"** — show the celebration animation and **"Payment Successful · Thank You!"** receipt.
7. Wrap up by showing the **Owner Admin Portal (`/admin`)** with today's revenue chart and printable tent-card QR generator.
